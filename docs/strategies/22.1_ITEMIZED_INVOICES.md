# M22 Phase 1 — Itemized Invoices

Status: Draft for review.
Parent: [M22](../milestones/22_LINE_ITEMS_DEVELOPER_API.md), Phase 1.
Spec: [LINE_ITEMS.md](../specs/LINE_ITEMS.md).

Goal: an invoice is built from lines, its total is their sum, and the lines show on every invoice surface.

Parallel work: §3's surfaces are path-scoped and can be split across subagents once §1 lands. §2 and §3 can run side by side.

## 1. [ ] Store lines and derive the total

1. [ ] Add the invoice line table and model: description, quantity, USD rate, a percentage flag, the lines it applies to, and position.
2. [ ] Compute the total from the lines, rounded to cents, and write it to the invoice's USD amount whenever its lines change.
   1. [ ] A percentage line applies to the sum of the lines it picks.
   2. [ ] A negative line reduces the total.
3. [ ] Migrate every existing invoice into one line: its description, quantity 1, and its amount as the rate.
   1. [ ] Verify each invoice's total is unchanged after the migration on dev.
4. [ ] Build lines in the invoice factory and the shared test invoice trait.

## 2. [ ] Edit lines in the app

1. [ ] Replace the single amount field on create and edit with a line editor: add, remove, and reorder lines; the total updates as lines change.
   1. [ ] Mark a line as a percentage and pick the lines it applies to.
   2. [ ] Keep every entered line on a validation error, with the error inline on its line.
   3. [ ] Derive the BTC amount and locked rate from the computed total per [RATES.md](../specs/RATES.md).
2. [ ] Validate lines on the server: at least one line, numeric quantity and rate, negative rates allowed, a percentage line picks at least one other line.
3. [ ] Rebuild assets.

## 3. [ ] Show lines on every surface

1. [ ] Issuer invoice view.
2. [ ] Public page.
3. [ ] Print.
4. [ ] Mail: invoice-ready, paid, and receipt messages.
5. [ ] Confirm lists, dashboard, and support views still show the total and nothing else changed.

## 4. [ ] Verify

1. [ ] [Agent] Targeted tests: line math with negative lines and percentage lines over chosen lines, rounding, migration, validation, and each surface rendering lines.
2. [ ] [Agent] Regression: invoice, partial payment, correction, manual payment, and notification tests pass unchanged.
3. [ ] [Agent] Full Sail suite.
4. [ ] [Subagent] Browser check of create and edit on desktop and mobile widths: keyboard-only line editing, labels read by a screen reader, no layout shift as lines are added.
5. [ ] [User] Review the line editor and the public, print, and mail output on dev.
6. [ ] [Agent] Record the dev verdict in the milestone doc.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Every behavior in [LINE_ITEMS.md](../specs/LINE_ITEMS.md) §1–§7 is verified on dev.
- [ ] The full suite is green, the dev verdict is recorded, and Phase 1 is checked off.
