# M22 Phase 1 — Itemized Invoices

Status: Approved; §1 active.
Parent: [M22](../milestones/22_LINE_ITEMS_DEVELOPER_API.md), Phase 1.
Spec: [LINE_ITEMS.md](../specs/LINE_ITEMS.md).

Goal: an invoice is built from lines, its total is their sum, and the lines show on every invoice surface.

Parallel work: §3's surfaces are path-scoped and can be split across subagents once §1 lands. §2 and §3 can run side by side.

## 1. [ ] Store lines and derive the total %<2981>

1. [x] Add the invoice line table and model: description, quantity, USD rate, a percentage flag, the lines it applies to, and position. %<2982>
2. [x] Compute the total from the lines, rounded to cents, and write it to the invoice's USD amount whenever its lines change. %<2983>
   1. [x] A percentage line applies to the sum of the lines it picks. %<2984>
   2. [x] A negative line reduces the total. %<2985>
3. [ ] Migrate every existing invoice into one line: its description, quantity 1, and its amount as the rate. %<2986>
   1. [ ] Verify each invoice's total is unchanged after the migration on dev. %<2987>
4. [ ] Build lines in the invoice factory and the shared test invoice trait. %<2988>

## 2. [ ] Edit lines in the app %<2989>

1. [ ] Replace the single amount field on create and edit with a line editor: add, remove, and reorder lines; the total updates as lines change. %<2990>
   1. [ ] Mark a line as a percentage and pick the lines it applies to. %<2991>
   2. [ ] Keep every entered line on a validation error, with the error inline on its line. %<2992>
   3. [ ] Derive the BTC amount and locked rate from the computed total per [RATES.md](../specs/RATES.md). %<2993>
2. [ ] Validate lines on the server: at least one line, numeric quantity and rate, negative rates allowed, a percentage line picks at least one other line. %<2994>
3. [ ] Rebuild assets. %<2995>
